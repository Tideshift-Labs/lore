## Upstream guidance (EpicGames/lore main)

The text below is upstream's AGENTS.md. In this fork the workspace and fork rules above come first;
upstream's skills under `.claude/skills/` still apply to Lore code.

**Read and follow `.claude/skills/lore-development/SKILL.md` at the start of every session,
before any other work.** Load it as a skill where your tool supports skills. It applies to
every task, routes to the standards and decision rules that govern the change, and defines
the review and submission steps. Read it again when the conversation is compacted or
resumed, and tell every subagent you brief to read it too.

When `context/team-workspace.md` exists, read it before non-trivial work and follow it. It
names the team's workspace for specs, plans, work items and decisions, and how to reach it.
Without it, work proceeds normally, and specs and plans stay in `context/`.
